    Edit and convert images privately, straight from your browser

    These tools cover the everyday image tasks without installing software: switch a photo between PNG, JPEG, WebP and AVIF, shrink it for the web with a quality slider, crop a region by dragging on the preview, add a text or logo watermark, adjust brightness, contrast and saturation, or clean the EXIF and GPS data that cameras embed in photos. Batch mode applies one conversion to many files at once.

    How to convert or compress an image

    1. Open Convert (or Compress & Resize) in the sidebar.
    2. Pick an image from your device — it is read locally.
    3. Choose the target format and quality, then download the result.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. Images are opened, edited and re-encoded inside your browser with the Canvas API. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored on a server.

    Does converting an image remove its metadata?

    Every tool re-encodes the pixels, which drops EXIF, GPS and other metadata from the output. The Strip Metadata tool makes that explicit for one or many files at once.

    Why does a converted JPEG look slightly different?

    JPEG is lossy: each re-encode applies a small amount of compression. Keep the quality slider high (90–100) to minimize visible loss, or use PNG for a lossless copy.
