Media servers store your films, shows, music and audiobooks and stream them to phones,
tablets and TVs — with metadata, subtitles, transcoding and per-user progress.

Transcoding is the main hardware question: if you need on-the-fly conversion for weak
clients, prefer an app with hardware acceleration support and budget CPU/RAM accordingly.
