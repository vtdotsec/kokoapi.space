Monitoring is what turns a hobby into a dependable setup: uptime checks with public status
pages, cron-job healthchecks, and metrics dashboards for hosts and containers.

Decide early between agent-based metrics collection (per-host agents) and lightweight
synthetic probes, and whether you need long-term retention for trend analysis.
