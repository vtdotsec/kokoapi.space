Password managers are usually the first serious service people self-host: the data is
highly sensitive, and the convenience of having your own vault server — rather than trusting
a hosted one — is easy to justify.

Look for active upstream sync clients (browser extensions and mobile apps matter most),
a license you are comfortable with, and SSO/LDAP support if you need team access.
