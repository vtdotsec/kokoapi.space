Self-hosted analytics gives you honest numbers about your sites without sending visitor
data to advertising giants. Modern tools use cookieless, privacy-friendly counting and
lightweight JavaScript snippets.

Heavier enterprise-style suites trade simplicity for funnels, session replay and
heatmaps — and proportionally more RAM.
