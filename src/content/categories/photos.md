Photo management is the emotionally hardest thing to outsource — and the most rewarding to
bring back. Modern self-hosted libraries offer automatic phone backup, deduplication,
albums, sharing, and often on-device or server-side face and object recognition.

Machine-learning features are what push RAM requirements up; check whether you can run them
on CPU or need a GPU.
