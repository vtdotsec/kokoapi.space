A notes or wiki system is a great second self-hosted service. Some entries in this category
are full collaboration servers with fine-grained permissions; others are personal,
file-based note stores you can open with any editor.

Consider whether you need real-time collaboration, an API, E2E encryption at rest, or
simply markdown files you can sync with Syncthing.
