File sync and sharing covers two needs: continuous peer-to-peer sync of your own folders,
and simple, expiring link-based file drops for other people.

Peer-to-peer sync keeps data on your devices and works even when you are offline; drop-style
tools are closer to "self-hosted WeTransfer" and typically need less configuration.
