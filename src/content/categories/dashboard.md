A dashboard is the front door of a homelab: a single page with links to your services,
bookmark groups, weather or system status. Most are configurable with a single YAML or JSON
file and are extremely lightweight.

Many support iframe/status widgets and some integrate OIDC or LDAP so family and friends can
have their own view.
