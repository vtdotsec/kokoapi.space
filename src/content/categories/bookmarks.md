Bookmark managers solve a real privacy gap: browser sync vendors see every link you save.
Self-hosted options range from minimal single-user link stores to full read-later systems
that archive page snapshots and index their text for search.

Some can watch a page and notify you when it changes — handy for price drops and docs.
