Secret Sender

Paste a note, a password or a short log, or upload a file up to 10 MB. You get a link that expires or burns after one read. Everything is encrypted in your browser before it is uploaded.

Encrypted end-to-end with AES-GCM in your browser. The decryption key stays in the link (after the #) and never reaches this server — only ciphertext is stored, and it is deleted when it expires or is read once.
