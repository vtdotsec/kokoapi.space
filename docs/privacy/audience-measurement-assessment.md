# Audience measurement assessment

This document records the product rationale and safeguards for first-party audience measurement on Tools and Repasses. It is an operational assessment for the site owner, not a legal opinion.

## Purpose and interest

Understand which tools receive use and whether visitors reach them through organic search, so the owner can prioritize maintenance and improve discovery. No advertising, sale of audience data, user profiling, or cross-site measurement is performed.

## Data and necessity

The collector receives a page path, a referrer origin reduced by the browser, and a campaign yes/no flag. It stores only date, site, sanitized path, a coarse traffic-source category, and aggregate pageview totals. The collector does not read or persist connection IP or user-agent headers; the reverse proxy does not forward those headers to the collector. Nginx access logs are disabled for the public Tools/Repasses servers and analytics routes.

No cookies, browser IDs, fingerprints, account IDs, or unique visitor counts are used. The raw referrer is classified in memory and discarded. Query strings and URL fragments are removed. Secret Sender reader links are excluded. Aggregates expire after 400 days. Traffic providers such as Cloudflare necessarily process connection metadata to deliver requests under their own policies.

## Impact and safeguards

Measurement is limited to pageview totals and broad source categories. It does not affect access to tools. File contents stay in the browser and are never sent to the collector. The privacy notice explains the measurement and its limits; a footer control disables future events for the current browser by saving a local-only opt-out flag. Clearing local site data clears that choice.

Some visitors may not expect audience measurement without a prompt. We reduce that impact with a clear notice and opt-out, no persistent tracking identifier, no person-level reporting, no profiling, no advertising, a first-party collector, and a 400-day maximum retention period.

The collector cannot estimate unique people or sessions and does not identify a person. A pageview may be repeated by the same visitor or generated by an automated browser. Visitors who block JavaScript or the collector are omitted.

## Review trigger

Reassess before adding identifiers, sessionization, advertising, account linking, cross-domain tracking, a third-party analytics processor, or a materially longer retention period. Reassess if the current measurement begins storing or exposing connection data.

## Guidance

The design follows the ANPD's guidance that legitimate interest may support audience measurement in some contexts when it is limited to aggregated patterns without user profiling or combining tracking mechanisms. This implementation should remain subject to the controller's context-specific review of LGPD requirements.
